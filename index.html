Beyond the Frame: Famous Paintings in Cinema

Curatorial Statement

Art and cinema have long shared a relationship through their ability to tell stories, communicate emotions, and capture the human experience. Beyond the Frame explores how famous paintings extend beyond their original canvases and find new life within the world of film. Featuring a selection of 5 influential artworks, this exhibition examines how filmmakers reference, recreate, and incorporate existing paintings into cinematic storytelling. Whether used to establish atmosphere, convey symbolism, or develop characters and narratives, these artworks take on new meanings when translated from still images into moving pictures. By exploring the connections between each painting and its cinematic appearance, the exhibition highlights the lasting influence of fine art on popular culture and visual storytelling. Ultimately, Beyond the Frame invites visitors to look beyond the canvas and discover how the relationship between art and cinema continues to shape the way we see, interpret, and experience images.

Artwork information and images retrieved from Getty, Yale LUX, and Rijksmuseum.
